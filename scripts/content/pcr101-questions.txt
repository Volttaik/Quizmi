Question 001. Peace Studies is best described as:

A. An interdisciplinary field that studies the causes of conflict and the conditions for peace
B. A course on international trade
C. A branch of military science only
D. The study of religion alone

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: It draws on sociology, political science, psychology, law and history.


Question 002. Peace Studies is called interdisciplinary because it:

A. Deals only with war
B. Ignores other subjects
C. Draws knowledge from many academic fields
D. Is taught only in one department

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Conflict has political, economic, social and psychological roots.


Question 003. The word "peace" in English derives from the Latin word:

A. Irenia
B. Salaam
C. Pax
D. Shalom

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Pax means a pact or agreement ending war.


Question 004. The Hebrew word for peace, "Shalom", implies:

A. Military victory
B. Only the absence of war
C. Wholeness, completeness and well-being
D. Silence

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Shalom is broader than ceasefire.


Question 005. The Arabic word "Salaam" means:

A. War
B. Peace, safety and submission to God's will
C. Justice only
D. Trade

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Shares a root with Islam.


Question 006. The Greek word for peace is:

A. Heping
B. Eirene
C. Pax
D. Shanti

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Eirene was also the Greek goddess of peace.


Question 007. "Shanti" is a Sanskrit term referring to:

A. Wealth
B. Military power
C. Inner peace and tranquillity
D. Revenge

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Common in Hindu and Buddhist thought.


Question 008. The Yoruba word commonly used for peace is:

A. Alaafia
B. Zaman lafiya
C. Udo
D. Eirene

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Alaafia means peace, health and well-being.


Question 009. The Igbo word for peace is:

A. Udo
B. Shanti
C. Alaafia
D. Salama

Explanation: Udo also connotes harmony.


Question 010. The Hausa expression "zaman lafiya" means:

A. Market day
B. Royal court
C. War time
D. Living in peace

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Zaman lafiya = peaceful coexistence.


Question 011. The simplest (traditional) definition of peace is:

A. The presence of justice for all
B. The absence of war or violent conflict
C. Total happiness
D. Economic growth

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: This narrow view was later expanded by Galtung.


Question 012. A major criticism of defining peace as merely "absence of war" is that it:

A. Is too broad
B. Ignores injustice, poverty and oppression that may exist without open fighting
C. Includes too much
D. Focuses on justice

Explanation: A society can be "quiet" yet deeply unjust.


Question 013. Johan Galtung is widely regarded as:

A. The founder of the UN
B. A military general
C. The founder of modern Peace Studies

Correct Answer: C

Question 014. Galtung founded the Peace Research Institute Oslo (PRIO) in:

A. 1990
B. 1919
C. 1959
D. 1945

Explanation: PRIO is among the first peace research institutes.


Question 015. The Journal of Peace Research was founded by Galtung in:

A. 1964
B. 1980
C. 2000
D. 1945

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: It became a leading academic peace journal.


Question 016. Peace as "a state of harmony, justice and well-being" reflects:

A. Military thinking
B. Realist theory only
C. A broad or positive view of peace
D. The narrow view of peace

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Positive peace includes justice.


Question 017. Peace Studies as an academic discipline developed mainly after:

A. The French Revolution
B. The year 2010
C. World War II
D. The end of the Cold War only

Question 018. The first chair of Peace Studies in Britain was established at the University of:

A. Bradford

A. MIT in 1970
B. Harvard in 1990
C. Manchester College, Indiana (1948)
D. Yale in 2005

Question 020. One major objective of Peace Studies is to:

A. Train soldiers for combat
B. Promote one religion
C. Understand the causes of conflict and find non-violent solutions

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: It seeks constructive conflict transformation.


Question 021. Peace Studies is normative because it:

A. Is purely descriptive
B. Avoids taking positions
C. Studies only numbers
D. Openly values peace and seeks to promote it

Explanation: It is committed to reducing violence.


Question 022. Peace Studies is also empirical because it:

A. Studies real-world conflicts with evidence

Question 023. Peace is described as "dynamic" because it:

B. Karl Marx
C. John Burton
D. Johan Galtung

Question 102. Negative peace is defined as:

A. The absence of direct (physical) violence
B. The presence of social justice
C. Inner happiness
D. Economic prosperity

Explanation: It means fighting has stopped.


Question 103. Positive peace is defined as:

A. Only the absence of war
B. Military occupation
C. A ceasefire
D. The presence of justice, equality and conditions that sustain harmony

Explanation: It removes structural violence.


Question 104. Galtung's 1969 article that set out negative and positive peace is titled:

A. Perpetual Peace
B. An Agenda for Peace
C. Human Needs Theory
D. Violence, Peace and Peace Research

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Published in the Journal of Peace Research.


Question 105. A ceasefire between two warring groups mainly produces:

A. Inner peace
B. Negative peace
C. Structural peace
D. Positive peace

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Violence stops but causes may remain.


Question 106. A society where all citizens have access to education, health and justice is moving towards:

A. Positive peace
B. Negative peace only
C. Direct violence
D. Structural violence

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Positive peace addresses root needs.


Question 107. Negative peace is often maintained through:

A. Inner reflection
B. Force, deterrence or law enforcement
C. Social justice
D. Reconciliation

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: It can be imposed.


Question 108. A major weakness of negative peace is that:

A. It is too expensive
B. It may hide unresolved grievances that can re-ignite conflict
C. It ends injustice
D. It is permanent

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Root causes remain.


Question 109. Positive peace is more sustainable because:

A. It tackles the root causes of conflict
B. It suppresses dissent
C. It uses more soldiers
D. It ignores grievances

Explanation: Addressing causes prevents recurrence.


Question 110. Negative peace corresponds to the absence of which type of violence?

Question 111. Positive peace corresponds to the absence of which type of violence?

Question 113. Which example best illustrates positive peace?

Question 115. The Global Peace Index is published by:

Question 967. Banditry in the North-West is linked to:

A. Strong security
B. Poverty, weak governance, arms proliferation and farmer-herder tensions
C. Abundance
D. High employment

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Zamfara, Katsina, Sokoto, Kaduna.


Question 968. Insecurity in Nigeria affects peace by:

A. Promoting development
B. Disrupting livelihoods, education and investment
C. Increasing food production
D. Encouraging tourism

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Wide-ranging effects.


Question 969. Election violence in Nigeria is linked to:

A. Low interest in politics
B. Ethnicity, religion, unemployment and the high stakes of power
C. Fair processes
D. Strong institutions

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Do-or-die politics.


Question 970. INEC stands for:

A. Independent National Electoral Commission
B. Independent Nigerian Ethics Commission
C. International Nigerian Electoral Committee
D. Internal National Election Council

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Conducts elections.


Question 971. The 12 June 1993 election, considered Nigeria's freest, was annulled by:

A. Muhammadu Buhari
B. General Ibrahim Babangida
C. Sani Abacha
D. Olusegun Obasanjo

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Winner was M.K.O. Abiola.


Question 972. Democracy Day in Nigeria has been celebrated on 12 June since:

A. 2010
B. 1993
C. 1999
D. 2019

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Honouring the 1993 election.


Question 973. Corruption affects peace in Nigeria by:

A. Diverting resources and deepening poverty and mistrust
B. Reducing crime
C. Creating equity
D. Improving services

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Fuels grievances.


Question 974. The EFCC was established in:

A. 2015
B. 1960
C. 2003
D. 1999

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Economic and Financial Crimes Commission.


Question 975. National unity in Nigeria can be strengthened through:

A. Hate speech
B. Ethnic favouritism
C. Marginalisation
D. Inclusive governance, equity, education and national integration programmes

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Belonging for all.


Question 976. The Unity Schools (Federal Government Colleges) were established to:

A. Train soldiers
B. Teach only religion
C. Promote one ethnic group
D. Bring together students from different ethnic and religious backgrounds

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Fostering national integration.


Question 977. Inter-ethnic marriages in Nigeria promote peace by:

A. Creating cross-cutting family ties
B. Increasing hostility
C. Promoting tribalism
D. Separating groups

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Bridges across groups.


Question 978. Hate speech in Nigerian politics undermines peace by:

A. Promoting unity
B. Building trust
C. Encouraging tolerance
D. Inflaming ethnic and religious tensions

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Especially on social media.


Question 979. Nigeria's diversity can be a source of strength when:

A. Exploited by elites
B. Suppressed
C. Managed with justice, equity and mutual respect
D. Ignored

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Unity in diversity.


Question 980. The 2014 National Conference in Nigeria discussed:

A. Space exploration
B. Restructuring, resource control and national unity
C. Football
D. Fashion

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Convened under President Goodluck Jonathan.


Question 981. "Restructuring" in Nigerian discourse generally refers to:

A. Abolishing states entirely
B. Rebuilding roads
C. Changing the flag
D. Reforming the federation to devolve more power and resources to states/regions

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Debated as a peace solution.


Question 982. State police has been proposed in Nigeria to:

A. Improve local security responsiveness
B. Centralise power further
C. Increase police brutality
D. Abolish all security

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Debate on decentralising policing.


Question 983. Amotekun, launched in 2020, is a security outfit of:

A. South-South states
B. North-Eastern states
C. South-Western states
D. The federal government

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Western Nigeria Security Network.


Question 984. Ebube Agu (2021) is a regional security outfit of:

A. Northern states
B. North-Central states
C. South-Eastern states
D. South-Western states

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Response to insecurity.


Question 985. The Civilian Joint Task Force (CJTF) in Borno emerged to:

A. Fight the federal government
B. Conduct elections
C. Promote banditry
D. Support security forces against Boko Haram

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Community self-defence.


Question 986. Which factor combination most threatens peace in Nigeria today?

A. Ethnicity, religion, resource control disputes and youth unemployment interacting
B. Peaceful elections and equity
C. Interfaith dialogue
D. Strong institutions and jobs

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Multiple reinforcing drivers.


Question 987. A peaceful Nigeria requires citizens to:

A. Support militias
B. Practise tolerance, respect diversity and reject violence
C. Spread rumours
D. Promote ethnic superiority

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Civic responsibility.


Question 988. Students can promote peace on campus by:

A. Joining cults
B. Exam malpractice
C. Spreading hate
D. Rejecting cultism, respecting diversity and resolving disputes peacefully

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Campus peace.


Question 989. The Arewa Consultative Forum represents:

A. Northern Nigerian socio-political interests
B. Yoruba interests
C. South-East interests
D. South-South interests

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Socio-cultural group.


Question 990. Ohanaeze Ndigbo is:

A. A Hausa group
B. The apex Igbo socio-cultural organisation
C. A militant group
D. A Yoruba group

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Represents Igbo interests.


Question 991. Afenifere is:

A. A northern group
B. An Igbo group
C. A Yoruba socio-political organisation
D. A Niger Delta militia

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Represents Yoruba interests.


Question 992. Socio-cultural organisations can promote peace by:

A. Supporting secession by force
B. Inciting violence
C. Engaging in dialogue and discouraging violence among their members
D. Spreading hate

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Influence on groups.


Question 993. Youth unemployment and resource control combined in the Niger Delta produced:

A. Higher oil output
B. Peace
C. Militancy, kidnapping and pipeline vandalism
D. Better environment

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Deprivation amid wealth.


Question 994. Religion and ethnicity combined in Jos and Kaduna crises show that:

A. Conflicts are simple
B. Identities never matter
C. Overlapping identities intensify conflicts
D. Peace is automatic

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Identity fault lines reinforce each other.


Question 995. (Revision) A youth joins a violent gang after years without work. This illustrates:

A. Youth unemployment as a threat to peace
B. Interfaith dialogue
C. Resource control
D. Positive peace

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Idle and frustrated youth are vulnerable.


Question 996. (Revision) Oil-producing communities demand more control over oil revenue. This is the issue of:

A. Religious extremism
B. Tribal marks
C. Resource control
D. Youth culture

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Niger Delta agitation.


Question 997. (Revision) Violence after a disputed election splits along ethnic and religious lines. This shows:

A. Good governance
B. Peace journalism
C. How ethnicity and religion are politicised in Nigeria
D. Positive peace

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Identity politics.


Question 998. (Revision) Which policy mix best promotes peace in Nigerian society?

A. Ethnic favouritism
B. Military force only
C. Banning religion
D. Inclusive governance, fair resource sharing, interfaith dialogue and youth employment

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Addresses the major drivers.


Question 999. (Revision) Nigeria's motto "Unity and Faith, Peace and Progress" suggests that peace in Nigeria depends on:

A. Separation of groups
B. Unity in diversity and shared commitment
C. Dominance by one group
D. Military rule

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: National values.


Question 1000. Nigeria's national anthem "Arise, O Compatriots" was readopted in 2024 to replace:

A. "Nigeria, We Hail Thee"
B. "God Save the Queen"
C. "One Nigeria"
D. "Unity Song"

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Originally adopted in 1978.
