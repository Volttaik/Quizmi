Question 001. The word "entrepreneur" is derived from the French word:

A. Entremets
B. Entrer
C. Entrepot
D. Entreprendre

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Entreprendre means to undertake.

Question 002. The French word "entreprendre" literally means:

A. To undertake
B. To employ
C. To borrow
D. To trade

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: An entrepreneur is one who undertakes a venture.

Question 003. Entrepreneurship is best defined as:

A. The process of creating value by identifying opportunities and bearing risk to exploit them
B. Working for a large firm for a salary
C. Managing government parastatals
D. Saving money in a bank

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: It combines opportunity, value creation and risk bearing.

Question 004. An entrepreneur is primarily a person who:

A. Only inherits a business
B. Avoids all forms of risk
C. Identifies opportunities and organises resources to exploit them at a risk
D. Works under strict supervision

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Opportunity recognition and risk taking are central.

Question 005. The economist who first popularised the term "entrepreneur" in economic literature was:

A. Karl Marx
B. John Keynes
C. Adam Smith
D. Richard Cantillon

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Cantillon described the entrepreneur as a risk bearer buying at certain prices and selling at uncertain prices.

Question 006. Richard Cantillon viewed the entrepreneur mainly as a:

A. Labourer
B. Government official
C. Risk bearer
D. Landlord

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: He buys at a known price and sells at an uncertain price.

Question 007. Jean-Baptiste Say described the entrepreneur as one who:

A. Collects rent
B. Only lends money
C. Shifts economic resources from lower to higher productivity
D. Only supplies labour

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Say stressed the coordinating role of the entrepreneur.

Question 008. Joseph Schumpeter saw the entrepreneur chiefly as an:

A. Innovator
B. Administrator
C. Imitator
D. Speculator only

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Schumpeter linked entrepreneurship to innovation.

Question 009. Schumpeter's concept of "creative destruction" refers to:

A. Destroying competitors' property
B. New innovations replacing old products and methods
C. Government seizure of firms
D. Closing loss-making factories by law

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Innovation renders older technologies obsolete.

Question 010. According to Schumpeter, which of these is NOT one of the forms of innovation?

A. Introduction of a new method of production
B. Introduction of a new product
C. Increasing taxes on imports
D. Opening of a new market

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Schumpeter listed products, methods, markets, sources of supply and organisation.

Question 011. Frank Knight associated entrepreneurial profit with the bearing of:

A. Rent
B. Uncertainty
C. Insurable risk
D. Labour

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Knight distinguished uninsurable uncertainty from measurable risk.

Question 012. In Knight's view, risk differs from uncertainty because risk is:

A. Unrelated to business
B. Never measurable
C. Measurable and insurable
D. Always avoidable

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Uncertainty cannot be calculated or insured.

Question 013. Israel Kirzner viewed the entrepreneur as someone who is:

A. Alert to unnoticed profit opportunities
B. A government planner
C. Primarily an inventor
D. A passive investor

Explanation: Kirzner emphasised alertness to market disequilibrium.

Question 014. Peter Drucker defined the entrepreneur as one who:

A. Prefers stability to growth

Question 015. David McClelland associated entrepreneurship with a high need for:
