Questions 001-063

Question 001. Scientific thought is best described as:

A. Laboratory work carried out only by chemists
B. A way of thinking that relies on questioning and evidence before belief
C. Memorising facts from textbooks
D. Acceptance of whatever authorities say

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Scientific thought is a method of reasoning, not a place or a set of facts.

Question 002. Which of the following is NOT a feature of scientific thought?

A. Logical reasoning
B. Reliance on evidence
C. Scepticism
D. Unquestioning acceptance of tradition

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Science doubts tradition until evidence supports it.

Question 003. The attitude of doubting claims until they are supported by evidence is called:

A. Mysticism
B. Dogmatism
C. Scepticism
D. Fatalism

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Scepticism is the organised doubt at the heart of science.

Question 004. According to scientific thinking, "common sense" knowledge should be:

A. Treated as sacred
B. Rejected without examination
C. Questioned and tested against evidence
D. Accepted because everyone believes it

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Common sense can be wrong, so science tests it.

Question 005. Science is NOT limited to the laboratory because:

A. Only social scientists use labs
B. It is fundamentally a method of inquiry applicable anywhere
C. Labs are too expensive
D. Laboratories were abolished

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: The method, not the location, defines science.

Question 006. The starting point of scientific thought is usually:

A. Asking a question about something observed
B. Writing the conclusion
C. Publishing a theory
D. Seeking political approval

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Inquiry begins with curiosity and questions.

Question 007. Empirical evidence refers to evidence derived from:

A. Revealed scripture only
B. Observation and experience through the senses
C. Dreams and visions
D. Personal opinion

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Empirical means based on sensory observation.

Question 008. Which statement reflects scientific thought?

A. "I will believe it when the evidence shows it."
B. "Questioning is disrespectful."
C. "It must be true because it feels right."
D. "It is true because my elders said so."

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Belief proportional to evidence is the scientific stance.

Question 009. A person who believes something simply because a famous person said it is relying on:

A. Systematic observation
B. Empiricism
C. Authority
D. Experimentation

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Appeal to authority is a non-scientific way of knowing.

Question 010. Knowledge based on tradition is accepted because:

A. It is mathematically proven
B. It is objective
C. It has been repeatedly tested
D. It has always been believed

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Tradition rests on custom, not testing.

Question 011. Rationalism as a way of knowing emphasises:

A. Reason and logic
B. Feelings and intuition
C. Revelation
D. Tradition

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Rationalism derives knowledge from reasoning.

Question 012. Empiricism as a way of knowing emphasises:

A. Sensory experience and observation
B. Pure reasoning without observation
C. Religious revelation
D. Authority figures

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Empiricism grounds knowledge in experience.

Question 013. Scientific thought combines which two ways of knowing?

A. Faith and superstition
B. Tradition and authority
C. Intuition and revelation
D. Reason and empirical observation

Correct Answer: D

Explanation: Science uses logic applied to observed evidence.

Question 014. The main difference between scientific and non-scientific knowledge is that scientific knowledge is:

A. Testable and open to correction
B. Held only by elites
C. Permanent and unchangeable
D. Based on revelation

Correct Answer: A

Explanation: Science accepts revision when new evidence appears.

Question 015. Critical thinking in science means:

A. Rejecting all knowledge
B. Agreeing with the majority
C. Carefully evaluating claims and evidence
D. Criticising scientists personally

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: Critical thinking is reasoned evaluation.

Question 016. "Organised scepticism" was identified as a norm of science by:

A. Auguste Comte
B. Robert K. Merton
C. Karl Marx
D. Bronislaw Malinowski

Correct Answer: B

Explanation: Merton listed communism, universalism, disinterestedness and organised scepticism.

Question 017. Which of Merton's norms says scientific claims should be judged by impersonal criteria, not the identity of the scientist?

A. Scepticism
B. Disinterestedness
C. Universalism
D. Communism

Explanation: Universalism rejects judging claims by race, nationality or status.

Question 018. Merton's norm of "communism" in science means:

A. Scientific findings belong to the whole community

C. Science must be done collectively in communes
D. Scientists must be socialists

Question 019. Disinterestedness as a scientific norm means scientists should:

A. Lose interest in their research

Question 020. A superstition differs from a scientific belief because a superstition:

A. Is always false

Question 021. Which of these is an example of scientific thought in daily life?

Question 033. Scientific thought developed partly as a reaction against:

Question 034. "Evidence-based" thinking means:

Question 038. Which of the following is a non-scientific way of knowing?
